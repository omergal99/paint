# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Paint is intended to be a welcoming, constructive project. Participants are
expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, explain disagreement
without personal attacks, and make space for people with different levels of
experience, backgrounds, and abilities.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, public disclosure of private
information, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
community channels, and project-related events. The maintainer will review
reports, protect the privacy of involved people where possible, and may edit,
close, or remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Please report a conduct concern privately to [@omergal99 on GitHub](https://github.com/omergal99)
and do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If the concern involves
the maintainer or private contact is unavailable, use GitHub's reporting/support
channels and avoid public disclosure while requesting a private route.

Reports will be acknowledged when reasonably possible and handled fairly. This
document is adapted from common open-source community practice; it is not a
substitute for local law or GitHub's platform policies.
